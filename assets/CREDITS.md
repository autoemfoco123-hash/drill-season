# Asset credits

Every texture, sky and scanned model in this folder comes from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and is released under **CC0** (public domain). They were resized, re-encoded and, for models, simplified with [glTF-Transform](https://gltf-transform.dev) to keep the game light. Models are stored as glTF JSON with separate JPEG textures; only the weathered variant of the fire hydrant is kept.
